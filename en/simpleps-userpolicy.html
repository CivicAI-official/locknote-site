This app is a password management app developed and operated by an individual. Information registered by users is stored on their device and is not sent to any external server. The developer strives to provide a stable service, but is not responsible for any damage caused by data loss or failures.

Article 2 (Features Provided)

This app is a tool for securely managing information such as accounts and passwords. Saved data is kept in a local storage area.

Article 3 (Paid Services)

This app does not display advertisements. Adding, editing and deleting passwords, To Dos and memos, restoring from encrypted backups, and importing data using CSV are available with the paid Premium Plan (¥100 per month, tax included, renewed automatically every month).
Users who sign up for the Premium Plan for the first time can use a 1-month free trial period. Sign-up is done in the app through Google Play. If the user cancels during the free trial period, no charge is incurred.
Unless the user cancels at least 24 hours before the end of the free trial period or the renewal date, the subscription is automatically renewed every month. Even if the user cancels, the Premium Plan can be used until the end of the period already paid for.
While the Premium Plan is not active (before signing up, or after cancellation or expiry), the app is view-only: the user can view and copy saved data, create encrypted backups and export data using CSV. Saved data is never deleted because the plan has ended.
Management, cancellation, re-subscription, etc. of the subscription shall follow the account settings of the app store used by the user.

Account information such as passwords, as well as To Dos and memos, is protected using encryption technology, and the developer makes every effort to prevent unauthorized use from outside.
However, even if every precaution is taken, the developer shall bear no responsibility whatsoever, even if information is leaked due to unauthorized access, hacking or the like against the user's device and the user suffers damage as a result.
