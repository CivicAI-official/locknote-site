First-time setup

  1. Choose your language

    When you open the app for the first time, a screen for choosing a language appears. Choose "English" and tap "Start with this language" at the bottom. You can change the language later on the Settings screen.

  2. Agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy

    Tap the blue text to read them. When you have finished reading, check the 2 boxes on the right and tap "Agree and Get Started".

  3. Choose a 6-digit PIN code (passcode)

    Enter the 6 numbers you will use to open the app, then tap "Next". To confirm, enter the same numbers again and tap "Set PIN". Tap the eye icon to check the numbers you entered.

  4. You're all set

    The password list screen appears. At first, nothing is saved in it.

⚠️ Please don't forget your PIN code
If you forget your PIN code, you will no longer be able to see the data in the app. Make sure you won't forget it, for example by writing it on paper and sharing it with your family.
2

Understanding the screens

There are 3 buttons at the very bottom of the screen. Tap one to switch to that screen.

🔒

Passwords

Keeps the ID (username) and password for each service.

☑️

To Do

A place to write down things you need to do. You can get a notification at the due date and time.

📝

Notes

A notepad where you can write freely. You can sort your notes with labels.

At the top right of the screen, the ⚙️ gear opens Settings, and the 🔒 lock button locks the app right away.

👀 When the app is "view only"
Before you start the free trial of the Premium Plan, or after you cancel, the app is "view only": you can view and copy your data, but you cannot add or edit anything. The app never shows ads.
3

Opening the app (Unlock)

  1. Enter your PIN code

    When you open the app, the "Unlock" screen appears. Enter the 6 numbers you chose, and the app opens automatically.

  2. You can also open it with your fingerprint or face

    If your fingerprint or face is registered on your phone, you can open the app without the PIN code by using the fingerprint icon at the bottom left.

⏱️ The app locks automatically
If you switch to another app and 60 seconds pass, the app locks automatically.
4

Adding a password

  1. Tap "+ Add New" at the bottom right

    The screen for adding a new entry opens.

  2. In "URL Name", enter a name that tells you which service it is

    For example: "Google", "Amazon", "XYZ Bank", and so on.

  3. Enter your "ID / Username"

    This is the ID, email address, phone number or similar that you use to log in.

  4. Enter the "Password"

    Tap the eye icon to show or hide the characters you typed. If you haven't decided on a password yet, you can create one automatically with the round arrow icon next to it.

  5. "Memo (optional)" can be filled in or left blank

    You can write things like secret words or notes about logging in.

  6. Tap "Save"

    The entry is added to the list. The list is sorted by name.

5

Viewing and copying a password

  1. In the list, tap the service you want to see

    The details screen opens.

  2. Show the password with the eye icon

    At first it is hidden as "●●●". Tap the eye icon to see the characters.

  3. Copy with the icon of two overlapping squares

    Tap the icon next to the ID, password or memo to copy it. Then use "Paste" on the login screen to enter it.

🔒 Copied text is erased after 30 seconds
So that other people can't see it, a copied password is automatically erased after 30 seconds. If you didn't make it in time, please copy it again.
6

Searching

  1. Tap "Search by URL, ID, or memo" at the top of the list

    You can now type text.

  2. Enter the word you want to search for

    Only the entries that contain the word you typed are shown right away.

💡 What you can search
The search looks in 3 places: the URL name (service name), the ID and the memo. You cannot search by the password itself.
7

Creating a secure password

  1. On the add or edit screen, tap the round arrow icon to the right of the password field

    With just one tap, a secure 16-character password is filled in automatically.

  2. Then just tap "Save"

    If you want a different one, tap the icon again.

🎲 The passwords it creates
16 characters that mix uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols. They are hard for other people to guess, so they are safe.
8

Editing and deleting

✏️ Editing

  1. On the details screen, tap the pencil icon at the top right

    The "Edit" screen opens.

  2. Change what you want to fix and tap "Save"

🗑️ Deleting

  1. On the details screen, tap the trash can icon at the top right
  2. On the confirmation screen, tap "Delete"

    If you change your mind, tap "Cancel".

⚠️ Deleted items cannot be brought back
Before deleting, please make sure you really want to delete it.
9

To Do (things to do)

You can write down things you need to do, such as booking a doctor's appointment or handing in documents. You can also have a notification sent to your phone at the due date and time.

  1. Tap "To Do" at the bottom, then tap "+ New To Do" at the bottom right
  2. Write what you need to do in "Title"

    In "Description (optional)", you can write things like what to bring (you can also leave it blank).

  3. Choose a "Due Date" and a "Time (optional)" if you like

    If you also set a time, you can turn on "Notification".

  4. If you want a reminder, turn on the "Notification" switch

    The first time you turn it on, your phone will ask you whether to allow notifications. Choose "Allow".

  5. Tap "Save"

    The list is divided into "Overdue", "Today", "Upcoming" and "Completed".

  6. When you're done, tap the box on the left to check it

    It moves to "Completed". Tap it again to put it back.

🔔 The notification icon
To Dos with notifications turned on show a bell icon on the right side of the list. Depending on your phone's power-saving settings, notifications may arrive a few minutes late.

To edit a To Do, tap it in the list. To delete it, tap the trash can icon at the top right of the screen that opens.

10

Notes

📝 Writing a note

  1. Tap "Notes" at the bottom, then tap "+ New Note" at the bottom right
  2. Write a title and the text

    If you have labels, you can add them by tapping a label below the title (you can add as many as you like).

  3. Tap the ✓ icon at the top right to save

🏷️ Sorting with labels

  1. On the Notes screen, tap the tag icon at the top right

    The "Labels" screen opens.

  2. Create a label with the "+" at the bottom right

    For example: "Family", "Health", "Hobbies". Tap the pencil to rename a label, or the trash can to delete it. Deleting a label does not delete your notes.

  3. Tap a label at the top of the list to show only the notes with that label

    With the search box at the top, you can search titles and text.

🔐 Notes and To Dos are encrypted too
What you write is encrypted and saved in your phone's secure storage area, just like passwords. Still, we recommend saving passwords on the "Passwords" screen, where you can use handy features such as copying and creating passwords automatically.

How your data is kept safe

🔢

Locked with a PIN code

People who don't know the 6-digit PIN code cannot open the app.

👆

Unlock with fingerprint or face

If your fingerprint or face is registered on your phone, you can also open the app that way.

⏱️

Auto-lock

If you switch to another app and 60 seconds pass, the app locks automatically.

🚫

Limit on wrong entries

If you enter the wrong PIN code 5 times, you cannot enter it for 1 minute. If you keep getting it wrong, the waiting time gets longer: 10 minutes, 15 minutes, and so on.

🔐

Data is saved encrypted

Passwords, To Dos and notes are encrypted and saved in your phone's secure storage area.

📋

Copies erased after 30 seconds

A copied password is automatically erased after 30 seconds.

🛡️ Your data stays only on your phone
The passwords, To Dos and notes you save are never sent to our servers or to the cloud. The app shows no ads, so it does not communicate with any advertising company either (for details, please see the Privacy Policy).
⚠️ Before changing phones
Because your data is only on your phone, if you lose your phone or switch to a new one, your data will be lost too. To be safe, use Save with CSV to keep a backup copy.

Saving and restoring with CSV ⭐ Import: Premium

You can export your saved passwords to a file (CSV format) to keep a backup copy. When you change phones, you can restore them by loading that file on your new phone.

  1. On the Settings screen, tap "CSV Management"
  2. To make a backup, tap "Export"

    Follow the instructions on the screen to choose where to save it.

  3. To restore, tap "Import"

    Choose the file you exported, then choose how to load it (Append / Overwrite / Replace All). Importing requires the Premium Plan (you can export at any time).

⚠️ Be careful with exported files
The file contains your passwords written as they are. Do not send it to anyone or put it anywhere others can see it. Delete it when you have finished using it.
