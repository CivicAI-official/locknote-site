🔏 Privacy Policy

This policy sets out how information is handled in LockNote.

This page is a translation provided for reference. If there is any difference between this translation and the Japanese version, the Japanese version takes precedence. View the Japanese version

1. Handling of Personal Information

Data registered by users (account names, passwords, memos, To Dos, etc.) is stored only on the device and is never sent to the developer's servers. The developer does not obtain or hold users' personal information.
Account information such as passwords, as well as To Dos and memos, is encrypted and stored in the device's secure storage area. The PIN code used to open the app is converted into a form that cannot be turned back into the original numbers (a hash value) before it is stored.
Fingerprint and face authentication uses the device's own features, and this app does not obtain any fingerprint or face information.

2. Provision to Third Parties

The developer never provides personal information saved in the app to any third party.

3. About Advertising

This app does not display ads. It contains no advertising SDK, and does not collect information for advertising or share it with third parties.

4. About Subscriptions (Premium Plan)

Payments for the Premium Plan are processed by Google Play. The developer does not obtain payment information such as credit card numbers.
To check whether the Premium Plan is active, the app asks Google Play for the purchase status each time it starts or returns to the foreground, and stores the result on the device.

5. About Notifications

Notifications that remind you of To Do due dates are scheduled and displayed within the device, and no information is sent outside the device. You can change the notification permission at any time from the device settings.

6. About Exporting Data (CSV and Encrypted Backup)

Files exported with CSV Export contain passwords recorded as they are. Please manage the save location and handling of exported files yourself.

Files created with Encrypted Backup are encrypted with a backup password you choose, and cannot be read by anyone who does not know that password. The file is saved only on your device or in a location you choose, and is never sent to the developer's servers. The backup password is never stored or sent, and the developer cannot know it, so a backup cannot be restored if you forget the password.

7. About Data Analysis

The developer does not analyze usage or collect crash information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised as necessary. If there are important changes, users will be notified within the app.
